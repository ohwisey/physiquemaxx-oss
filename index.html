The idea worth stealing

Ask AI to "rate this" and it drifts. Same photo, different answer. Here is the fix.

1See

The model looks at the photo. Nothing else. Just describe what is there.

2Score against a fixed rubric

Grade every photo on the same written checklist, at temperature 0. Same input, same score, every time.

3Narrate

Turn the numbers into plain, human feedback. What is working, what to do next.

Split the job in three and each part gets simple. That is what makes AI repeatable instead of random.
